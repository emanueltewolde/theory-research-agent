This is the authoritative compact portfolio of research directions. It records both what is being pursued and what should not be repeated without new evidence. Exact proofs, experiments, and literature findings belong in linked artifacts.

## Classification

- **Activity:** `active`, `parked`, `blocked`, or `closed`.
- **Assessment:** `promising`, `uncertain`, `attempted-unresolved`, `weak`, `succeeded`, or `failed`.

Activity says whether work should proceed now. Assessment says what current evidence implies. Do not infer one from the other: a promising direction may be parked, and a failed direction remains useful durable knowledge.

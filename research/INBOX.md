# Research Inbox

- **Contract revision:** 0
- **Last integrated run:** none
- **Last triage:** not yet performed
- **Inbox authority:** current integration authority

This is the designated intake for off-task findings, possible lemmas, experiments, literature leads, model concerns, blockers, infrastructure needs, and human-alignment questions. It is not a second direction portfolio or task queue. Every item must eventually be pursued, merged, deferred, discarded, or resolved with a reason.

## Controlled values

- **Kind:** `idea`, `question`, `lemma`, `experiment`, `literature lead`, `model concern`, `blocker`, `infrastructure`, or `human alignment`.
- **Disposition:** `new`, `pursue`, `merged`, `deferred`, `discarded`, or `resolved`.
- **Uncertainty class for alignment items:** `safe provisional`, `consequential but deferrable`, `dangerous now`, or `project-blocking`.

Human-alignment items also record the provisional assumption, dependent conclusions, safe continuation horizon, affected branch, and eventual human answer. Dangerous uncertainty pauses the affected branch rather than stopping unrelated useful work.
